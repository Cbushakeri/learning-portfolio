# learning-portfolio


What emerging technology did you choose? How is AI being used to support this technology?
I chose wearable health devices as an emerging technology. AI is used to analyze the health data collected through sensors and turn it into useful information. AI can identify patterns in heart rate, sleep quality, physical activity, and other health measurements. For example, the Oura Ring uses AI to monitor sleep, track activity levels, and detect changes in health patterns that may indicate potential concerns. AI can also provide personalized recommendations based on an individual's habits and health data. By analyzing information in real time, AI makes wearable health devices more effective and personalized.
As someone who works in user experience, I find this technology especially interesting because it gives people immediate feedback about their behaviors and health habits. This can encourage user to choose a healthier lifestyle.

Are there any concerns with using AI for this application?
There are several concerns related to the use of AI in wearable health devices, including accuracy, security, privacy, and the possibility that users may become overly dependent on the technology. These devices are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Some users may rely so heavily on the information provided by wearable devices that they skip seeking healthcare professionals.
One area that I find particularly interesting is the role of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) in protecting personal health information. While insurance companies generally cannot access data from wearable devices without a user's permission, people may voluntarily share their information through wellness programs, fitness applications, or other third-party services without fully understanding how that data may be used. This raises important questions about privacy and data security.
